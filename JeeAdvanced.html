Important Announcements

Round 5 Allotment and Deadline for JoSAA 2026

JoSAA Round 5 allottment has been announced on the JoSAA portal. Candidates who have been allotted a seat for the first time in Round 5, must upload and submit all the necessary documents and pay the Seat Acceptance Fee by 5pm on July 20, 2026 (Monday)

Please note that the above steps are mandatory in order to confirm your allotted seat.

Withdrawal from JoSAA 2026

The deadline to withdraw from JoSAA 2026 is 14th July 2026 at 5pm (Tuesday).No withdrawal requests will be accepted after this deadline.

Round 4 Allotment and Deadline for JoSAA 2026

JoSAA Round 4 allottment has been announced on the JoSAA portal. Candidates who have been allotted a seat for the first time in Round 4, must submit their willingness (Freeze/Float/Slide), upload and submit all the necessary documents and pay the Seat Acceptance Fee by 5pm on July 13, 2026 (Monday).

Please note that the above steps are mandatory in order to continue in JoSAA in subsequent rounds.
